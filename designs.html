📚 Real apps this is built on

The "Good afternoon, Daniel" treatment comes from how Readwise names each email digest. Craft Docs Paper-cream background, warm shadows, low-key dividers. v2 Paper, refined with Sunrise warmth
What changed from v1
  • Mic is now a warm coral-terracotta gradient with a soft breathing animation. No more dark brown dot against cream — it now reads as inviting.
  • Greeting uses Fraunces italic with a terracotta accent on your name. More personality, same restraint.
  • Background is a slightly warmer cream (less grey, more honey).
  • Active goal row has a soft honey wash so your current task is visually obvious without being loud.
  • "Together" goals get a sage chip — so you and Chardae's shared goals stand out from solo ones at a glance.

01 Greeting with time of day — switches between morning / afternoon / evening automatically. Pattern borrowed from Readwise emails and Craft's home.
02 Scoreboard as a single card with thin rules — Mercury Bank's balance panel. Less visual noise than three separate pills.
03 Active row has a soft honey wash — not loud. Points your eye to "what's next" without yelling. Streaks does this on the current day.

05 Mic fix: warm gradient circle with a gentle breathing animation. Terracotta → coral — reads as "friendly tool", not "sinister button". Shadow spreads in the same warm tone so it feels native to the paper.
